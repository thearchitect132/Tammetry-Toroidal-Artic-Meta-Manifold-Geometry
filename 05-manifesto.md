## 1. Statement of Purpose

Tammetry is founded to establish a new branch of mathematics dedicated to the study of **recursive toroidal manifolds** and the **non-commutative operator algebra** that governs their identity, structure, restoration, direction, and expansion.

**What Tammetry Is:**
- A formal mathematical discipline
- A rigorous framework for recursive geometry
- A unifying substrate for disparate theories

**What Tammetry Is Not:**
- Physics (applied to specific systems)
- Metaphysics (speculative beyond mathematics)
- Cosmology (claims about the universe)
- Spirituality (narrative meaning)
- Philosophy (interpretive claims)

Tammetry is mathematics. This distinction is foundational.

- Tammetry: operators as fundamental → curvature from commutators

### To Category Theory
Tammetry is **compatible with** category theory. Tammetric operators can be viewed as functors, and manifolds as objects in a category.
