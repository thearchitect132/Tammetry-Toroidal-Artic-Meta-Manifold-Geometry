# TAMMETRIC KNOWLEDGE TAXONOMY

How Tammetry organizes all human knowledge.

---

## 1. The Failure of Classical Taxonomies

Traditional knowledge taxonomies divide disciplines into categories:
- Natural Sciences (physics, chemistry, biology)
- Social Sciences (economics, psychology, sociology)
- Humanities (philosophy, literature, history)
- Applied Sciences (engineering, medicine, technology)
- Arts (music, visual arts, performance)

**Problems with this approach:**
- Disciplines don't fit neatly into boxes
- Boundaries are arbitrary and contested
- Interdisciplinary work is orphaned
- No unified organizing principle
- Hierarchies are imposed, not emergent

**Tammetry's solution:**
Replace category-based taxonomy with **domain-recursive classification**.

## 2. The Five Knowledge Domains

Every human knowledge field can be classified by its relationship to the five Tammetric domains:

### Identity Domain (𝕀)
**Essence, meaning, being, invariants**

Core questions:
- What is this fundamentally?
- What persists unchanging?
- What is its true nature?

Primary fields:
- Philosophy (metaphysics, ontology)
- Theology and spirituality
- Ethics and values
- Foundational mathematics
- Pure logic

### Structural Domain (𝕊)
**Form, pattern, system, framework, organization**

Core questions:
- How is this organized?
- What patterns does it exhibit?
- What rules govern its structure?

Primary fields:
- Mathematics (geometry, algebra, analysis)
- Logic and formal systems
- Linguistics and grammar
- Architecture and design
- Computer science (algorithms)

### Restorative Domain (𝕽)
**Stability, correction, equilibrium, coherence maintenance**

Core questions:
- How does this maintain stability?
- How are errors corrected?
- What prevents collapse?

Primary fields:
- Law and justice
- Medicine and healthcare
- Quality control and standards
- Ecology and conservation
- Psychology (healing and integration)

### Directional Domain (𝔻)
**Causality, flow, prediction, trajectory, evolution**

Core questions:
- What causes this?
- Where is this heading?
- How do things progress?

Primary fields:
- Physics and classical mechanics
- Economics and market dynamics
- Neuroscience and consciousness
- History and narrative
- Systems dynamics

### Expansion Domain (𝔼)
**Growth, innovation, creativity, paradigm shift, emergence**

Core questions:
- How does this grow and evolve?
- What new possibilities emerge?
- How are breakthroughs made?

Primary fields:
- Art and aesthetics
- Technology and engineering
- Biology and evolution
- Culture and civilization
- Entrepreneurship and innovation

### Convergence (Integration)

## 5. The Recursion Depths

Knowledge can be classified by **level of abstraction**:

| Depth | Level | Characteristics | Examples |
|-------|-------|-----------------|----------|
| n=0 | Concrete | Directly observable, empirical | Chemistry, biology, engineering |
| n=1 | Abstract | Theoretical frameworks | Physics, economics, psychology |
| n=2 | Meta-theoretical | About the theories themselves | Philosophy of physics, epistemology |
| n=3 | Civilisational | About civilization's knowledge structure | Philosophy, theology, systems theory |
| n=∞ | Tammetric | Recursive toroidal ground | Tammetry itself |

### Depth Transitions

Movement between depths:
- **n=0 → n=1** — theory building (observation → law)
- **n=1 → n=2** — meta-theory (law → understanding)
- **n=2 → n=3** — wisdom (understanding → civilisation)
- **n=3 → n=∞** — unity (civilisation → Tammetry)

The field begins coupling with neighboring domains.

Example: Tammetry (All domains, All horizons, n=∞)
