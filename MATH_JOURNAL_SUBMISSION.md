## MATHEMATICS VERSION: KEY SECTIONS

### Abstract (Mathematical Focus)

Tammetry introduces a non-commutative operator algebra on recursive toroidal manifolds with applications to geometry and topology. We establish seven axioms, seven field equations, and derive curvature from operator commutation relations rather than metric tensors. The five-operator algebra exhibits mixed-commutative structure with Identity-centrality and Restoration-Expansion duality. We prove mathematical consistency, topological closure, and provide a novel alternative to classical differential geometry. Key theorem: curvature emerges from $[\mathbb{S}, \mathbb{D}]$ rather than metric derivatives. We demonstrate that classical Riemannian geometry is a special case of Tammetric geometry. Applications to systems theory and knowledge organization are sketched.

**Keywords:** operator algebra, recursive manifolds, toroidal geometry, non-commutative geometry, topological closure, geometric foundations
